## Market Position

**"Security report card for your business email and domain in 60 seconds."**

Positioned against:
- Enterprise tools: too complex and expensive for freelancers
- Consumer tools: not business-focused, miss domain security
- Manual audits: too expensive, one-off

**Competitive advantages:**
- Opinionated simplicity — not 100 features, just the vital ones
- Fast iteration — solo dev, ship weekly
- Transparent pricing — no enterprise sales
- Personal touch — direct support, community engagement

---

## Launch Sequence

### Week 1: Warm Audience
- Friends & family, LinkedIn connections
- Email signature link
- **Goal:** 50 free scans

### Week 2: Community Launch
- **ProductHunt** — Tuesday/Wednesday, engage all day
- **Reddit:** r/smallbusiness, r/freelance, r/cybersecurity, r/SideProject
  - Format: "I built this to solve my own problem..."
- **Hacker News:** Show HN — transparent about journey

### Month 2–3: Content Marketing
SEO content targeting:
- "How to check if your email has been hacked" (TOFU)
- "Setting up DMARC for small businesses" (MOFU)
- "Best security practices for freelancers" (TOFU)
- "What to do after a data breach" (BOFU)

Distribution: blog → Medium / Dev.to / Hashnode → Twitter/LinkedIn weekly

### Month 4–6: Partnerships
- Freelance communities (Indie Hackers)
- Domain registrars (co-marketing)
- Password manager communities

### Month 7+: Paid Acquisition (if LTV:CAC > 3)
- Google Ads: "email breach check", "domain security scan"
- LinkedIn/Twitter ads (small budget tests)

---

## Conversion Funnel

```
Visitor lands
  → Runs free scan (email + optional domain)
  → Sees security score + top 5 findings
  → Prompted to create account for full report
  → Gets detailed playbooks (after signup)
  → Offered Pro for monitoring
```

### Email Drip (Post Free Scan)
- Day 1: "Your security score breakdown"
- Day 3: "Here's how to fix your top issue"
- Day 7: "New breach detected — upgrade for alerts"
- Day 14: "50% of users improved their score — here's how"

### In-App Upsells
- Paywall on detailed playbooks
- "Upgrade to export PDF report"
- "Get weekly monitoring for £10/month"

## KPIs to Track

| Category | Metric |
|---|---|
| Acquisition | Website visitors, free scans started/completed, email signups |
| Activation | % complete full scan, % create account, % read ≥1 playbook |
| Revenue | Free→Pro conversion rate, MRR, ARPU, LTV |
| Retention | Monthly churn (target <5%), NPS, active subscriber count |
